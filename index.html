iPhone · TrueDepth & LiDAR

Turn your iPhone's depth camera into a living visual instrument.

ZPTH reads the depth in front of you and paints it in real time. Point it at a face, a room, or your hand and watch the depth become light, contour lines, plasma, chrome, false-colour infrared, a mosaic, or pixel-art dither.

NDI streaming · Pro

Your iPhone becomes a depth-camera source for your desktop.

Turn on NDI in Settings to broadcast your live preview to NDI receivers — OBS, TouchDesigner, vMix and more — on your local network.

Private by design

No accounts, no servers, no analytics, no tracking.

Your camera frames are processed on the device and never leave it — unless you save a photo or video to your own library, or turn on NDI to send the preview to your own computer on your local network.

Microphone

Only the audio-reactive Pulsar and Cymatic modes use it. Off by default, analysed on-device, never recorded or transmitted.
